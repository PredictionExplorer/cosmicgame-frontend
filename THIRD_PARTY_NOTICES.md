The share cards (Open Graph images) embed font subsets generated by
`npm run og:fonts` from the google/fonts repository at a pinned commit:

- `assets/fonts/NotoSansSC-700.subset.ttf`, `assets/fonts/NotoSansSC-400.subset.ttf`,
  `assets/fonts/NotoSansTC-700.subset.ttf`, `assets/fonts/NotoSansTC-400.subset.ttf`,
  `assets/fonts/NotoSansHK-700.subset.ttf`, `assets/fonts/NotoSansHK-400.subset.ttf`,
  `assets/fonts/NotoSansKR-700.subset.ttf`, `assets/fonts/NotoSansKR-400.subset.ttf`,
  `assets/fonts/NotoSansJP-700.subset.ttf`, and `assets/fonts/NotoSansJP-400.subset.ttf`
  are weight-700 and weight-400 subsets of Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans TC, Noto Sans HK,
  Noto Sans KR, and Noto Sans JP (the Simplified, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Korean, and
  Japanese cuts of Noto Sans CJK); see
  [assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt).
- `assets/fonts/Inter-400.subset.ttf` and `assets/fonts/Inter-500.subset.ttf` are
  subsets of Inter; see [assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt).
- `assets/fonts/JetBrainsMono-500.subset.ttf` is a subset of JetBrains Mono; see
  [assets/fonts/OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt).

All of them remain licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The share cards also rasterize `ClashDisplay-Medium.ttf` and `ClashDisplay-Bold.ttf`
from `public/fonts/ClashDisplay/fonts/`, unmodified. Clash Display is designed by the
Indian Type Foundry and distributed through Fontshare under the ITF Free Font License
(<https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl>), the same terms under which the site
serves it as a web font.

The site serves `public/fonts/noto-sans-kr/NotoSansKR-punctuation.ttf`, the five
ASCII marks `! , . : ?` cut with `hb-subset` from `assets/fonts/NotoSansKR-400.subset.ttf`,
as the web fallback of the Korean display punctuation alias (`styles/global.css`).
It remains licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see
[assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt).

The site also serves two display-alias web fonts (`styles/global.css`) that
`npm run og:fonts` cuts from the checked-in share-card subsets (`WEB_FONT_CUTS` in
`scripts/build-og-fonts-core.ts`):

- `public/fonts/display-figures/Inter-figures.woff2`, the digits `0`–`9` and `#` of
  `assets/fonts/Inter-500.subset.ttf`, a subset of Inter; see
  [assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-Inter.txt).
- `public/fonts/noto-sans-cjk/NotoSansCJK-digits.woff2`, the digits `0`–`9` of
  `assets/fonts/NotoSansSC-700.subset.ttf`, a subset of Noto Sans SC; see
  [assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt).

Both remain licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

The Japanese and Korean white paper PDFs also embed regular (400) and bold (700)
subsets of Noto Sans JP and Noto Sans KR. `npm run white-paper:pdf` generates these
from the same pinned google/fonts sources, using each complete paper's glyph set.
The embedded fonts retain the SIL Open Font License 1.1 linked above.

The bundled `assets/fonts/Onest-500.subset.ttf` file is a weight-500 subset of
the Onest variable font (its Cyrillic and Vietnamese repertoires, serving the
Ukrainian and Vietnamese share-card titles), generated by the same `npm run og:fonts`
pipeline from the google/fonts repository. It remains licensed under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1; see [assets/fonts/OFL-Onest.txt](assets/fonts/OFL-Onest.txt).

Nothing in the project's CC0 dedication overrides or relicenses these
third-party materials.
